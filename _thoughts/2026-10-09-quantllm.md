  Why hasn’t agentic trading yet succeeded in using agents to discover factors and build combo strategies that deliver strong returns, high Sharpe ratios, and effective drawdown control in live, long-only enhanced-index strategies?

  I think the main reason is that, no matter how many insights or priors we give an agent before asking it to analyze or make judgments, financial markets have such a low signal-to-noise ratio that its conclusions tend to feel like overgeneralizations based on incomplete information. In other words, “a truth left half-told is no better than nonsense.”

  After running more live strategies and experiments, I’ll write another article to briefly explain my thoughts.
