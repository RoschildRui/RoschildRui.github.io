  Came across a Douyin summary of a conversation between Sun Ge and the "Lean-Muscle Godfather":

  > In the future, any theory, invention, asset, or person that AI cannot read will gradually lose its meaning.
  >
  > If AI cannot understand your theory, it is as if your theory never existed.
  >
  > If you as a person cannot be read by AI, you are as good as dead.

  I found it quite interesting. Human-to-human communication is not reusable, and its cost is far higher than communicating with AI. Seen in this light, ByteDance's documentation culture was actually quite forward-looking.
